About this item

This UTI tape is a gas-tight portable liquid level sensor designed for restricted oil-water measurement. Equipped with an anti-static tape and spark-proof construction, it guarantees certified safety operation in Zone 0, Zone 1, and Zone 2 environments.

Product Description Technical Specifications Client Reviews (0)

In complex marine tankers and bulk storage facilities, knowing the exact volume of your cargo while maintaining safety protocols is paramount. The Bushtorm certified Ullage Temperature Interface (UTI) Tape represents the peak of portable liquid level indicators. Designed to work hermetically, these systems prevent hazardous gas leaks from escaping while taking critical measurements.

What is a UTI Tape?

A UTI Tape is an intrinsically safe, portable measurement device that collects three key metrics in a single drop: Ullage (the distance between the cargo surface and the tank top), Temperature profile, and the Interface level between oil and water phases. This 3-in-1 measurement system drastically reduces operating time and exposure risks.

Key Features

  • 3-in-1 Measurement: Obtain ullage distance, multiple-point temperature profiles, and water-oil interface levels instantly.
  • Hermetically Sealed Probes: Zero-gas leakage design allows safe operation on restricted and fully closed tanker structures.
  • Intrinsically Safe Design: Constructed with explosion-proof material fully certified for explosive Zone 0 atmospheres.
  • High Accuracy: Equipped with state-of-the-art platinum RTD temperature sensors ensuring accurate thermal calibration.
  • Durable Tape Construction: Utilizes anti-static, chemical-resistant steel tape coated with high durability fluoropolymer protective outer layer.
